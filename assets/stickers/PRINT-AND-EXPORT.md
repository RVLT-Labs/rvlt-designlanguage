# Print and export notes

The SVGs are editable artwork masters. They are ready to display digitally, but they do not contain printer-specific cut paths, bleed, separations or embroidery instructions.

## Physical stickers

Start a proof around 50–75 mm wide, then inspect the smallest gaps and lettering at actual size. This is a design starting point, not a tested production minimum. Ask the printer to establish the cut contour, safe margin, bleed and colour profile for the chosen stock.

The cream border is part of the design. Decide with the printer whether the screen shadow should be omitted or printed inside the physical cut shape. Do not automatically treat the SVG's rectangular viewBox as the cut contour. Confirm the cream and bright fills on the final material; transparent stock needs a deliberate white-ink plan.

## Embroidered patches

The patch designs refer visually to stitched labels, but the SVGs are not machine stitch files. Ask the maker to simplify small decorative stitches, dots, holes and letter gaps as needed. Review a physical sample, particularly Crew, Standby and Show time.

## PNG or presentation exports

Keep the SVG as the master. Export a PNG at the required final pixel size, or a multiple of it for a high-density display. Preserve aspect ratio and transparency. If a tool displays transparency against white, preview cream line artwork on a dark background before concluding that the image is empty.

Use `preview/contact-sheet.png` for asset selection only; its labels and background are not individual app assets. The figures in `docs/images/` illustrate the guide and should not replace the SVG masters in the app.
